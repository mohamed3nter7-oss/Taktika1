Recommendation

Take 2a, four roles with the two unavailable ones omitted entirely. A disabled card is a dead end for exactly the person it excludes, and at registration there is no account yet to notify, so anticipation has nothing to convert into — the roadmap belongs on the marketing page or behind a notify-me capture, not in the form. The permanence warning sits directly above the submit button rather than in a dialogue afterwards, and it names the chosen role so it cannot be skimmed as boilerplate. When a role change invalidates an already-valid date, the message attaches to the role grid the user just touched and offers the two real exits, while the date field carries a factual age line instead of a red error it did not earn.

Primitives — naming the gaps rather than improvising them
RoleSelectCard — genuine gap

Radio semantics, selected state, unavailable state. Composed here from a tokened button plus RoleBadge compact, so role colour stays inside the only component licensed to carry it and selection is carried by the accent border and fill. Needs to exist as a primitive before build.

No date field in the system

Forms are Input, Select, Textarea only. Shown as a text Input with tabular figures and marked placeholder — substitute the repository's date input. Privacy line kept on the field: the profile shows age, never date of birth.

No checkbox, so no acknowledgement

Which the brief wants anyway. The permanence block is a statement above the submit, on the sunken surface with a strong hairline and a lock glyph, no semantic colour.

Country and city are one row

Two dependent selects side by side at 1280, stacked at 390. Both loading states are shown: countries load on mount in state 1, cities load after the country lands in state 3.
