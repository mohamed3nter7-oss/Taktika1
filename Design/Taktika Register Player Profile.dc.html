Player profile

Recommendation

Ship 3a, the dropdown, and build the pitch for profile editing instead. Registration is the one moment the user is least willing to spend time and most likely to be on a 390px screen, where twelve 44px hit targets on a pitch either overlap or shrink below the touch minimum, and the exclusion rule reads more clearly as a struck-through row than as a dimmed marker. The pitch also costs a direction ruling — it has to stay dir="ltr" in Arabic like the position codes do, because mirroring it moves LW to the right of the screen — and it spends the system's one-per-page pitch line, which is why the rail drops its rule in 3b. In profile editing none of that is a problem: the user has time, the screen is usually desktop, and the spatial picker is genuinely the better tool for the field that scout search depends on.

Primitives and placeholders
Select needs one prop, not a new primitive

A per-option disabled flag, so the secondary list can render the primary position as unavailable rather than accepting it and rejecting it afterwards. State 9 draws the open listbox to show the treatment: muted row, "primary" note, not selectable, still readable.

ChoiceGroup — genuine gap

Preferred foot is three short values and a dropdown for three values is a needless dropdown. Composed here as 44px radio tiles with the accent-subtle selected treatment. Same primitive would serve the pitch option's primary/secondary pass toggle.

Position list is placeholder

Five of twelve shown — GK, LB, CB, CM, ST — and marked in the field helper and at the foot of the list. Codes stay Latin in both locales through PositionChip, which already isolates direction. The pitch draws the remaining seven as dashed placeholders with placeholder coordinates.

No retreat, and it is not hidden

No back button anywhere on the screen. Steps 1 and 2 in the rail carry the accent check with no chevron, no hover and no link, and one muted line states that completed steps are locked. The signed-in row under the heading is the standing proof that the account already exists.
